About

I hold an M.S. in Environmental Science & Engineering from South China Normal University, and I am currently a researcher at Guangxi Research Institute of Metrology & Test, specializing in quantitative measurement and uncertainty analysis of forest and agricultural carbon sinks.

My expertise covers soil-ecosystem material cycling processes and their regulatory mechanisms: nutrient coupling, solute transport, carbon budget quantification, and optimized management.
